# Product Roadmap

# Rakshak
### Your AI Companion for Safe Digital Banking

Version: 1.0
Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of Rakshak.

It outlines product milestones, technical objectives, AI improvements, and future capabilities while maintaining a clear direction for contributors.

The roadmap is a planning document and may evolve as the project grows.

---

# Vision

To become India's most trusted AI-powered financial safety companion by helping every digital banking user identify, understand, and avoid financial scams before money is lost.

---

# Roadmap Principles

Every release should improve at least one of the following:

- Financial Safety
- AI Accuracy
- User Experience
- Accessibility
- Performance
- Privacy
- Reliability

Features should solve real user problems rather than increase feature count.

---

---

# Out of Scope

The following are not planned for the current roadmap:

- Online banking
- Payment processing
- UPI transaction execution
- Financial account management
- Antivirus functionality
- General device security tools

Rakshak focuses exclusively on financial scam prevention and recovery.

# Summary

Rakshak will evolve through incremental, user-focused releases that prioritize financial safety, explainable AI, accessibility, and privacy. Every milestone builds upon a stable foundation while maintaining the project's mission of protecting users from digital financial fraud.
