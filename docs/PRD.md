# 1. Product Vision

Rakshak is an AI-powered financial safety companion that protects first-time digital banking users before, during, and after scam attempts through real-time scam detection, multilingual AI guidance, proactive financial education, and guided recovery.

Rather than acting as just another security application, Rakshak serves as a trusted digital companion that helps users confidently navigate digital banking while reducing the risk of financial fraud.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Millions of users are entering digital banking for the first time.

Although digital payments have become easier, financial scams have also become increasingly sophisticated.

Common scams include:

- Fake UPI collect requests
- Fake KYC update messages
- Loan scams
- QR code scams
- OTP theft
- Phishing websites
- Fraudulent customer care numbers
- Fake bank representatives
- WhatsApp scam messages
- Screen-sharing scams

Most first-time users cannot identify these scams.

Existing security applications often provide technical warnings that are difficult for non-technical users to understand.

Rakshak aims to bridge this gap through AI-powered detection combined with simple explanations in local languages.

# 5. Goals

Rakshak aims to:

- Detect financial scams before users become victims
- Warn users in real time
- Explain scams in simple language
- Prevent panic through guided assistance
- Increase financial literacy
- Encourage safe digital banking habits

---

# 6. Non Goals

Rakshak is NOT:

- A banking application
- A payment application
- An antivirus
- A password manager
- A VPN
- A general cybersecurity toolkit

The focus is strictly financial safety.

# 9. Core Features

---

## Feature 1

### AI Scam Guardian

Background monitoring engine.

Monitors

- SMS
- WhatsApp notifications
- Gmail notifications
- Bank notifications
- UPI notifications
- Unknown links
- Suspicious messages

Provides

- Risk score
- Scam category
- Gujarati explanation
- English explanation
- Recommended action

---

## Feature 2

### Scam Call Guardian

Protects users during suspicious calls.

Capabilities

- Incoming call overlay
- Scam awareness reminder
- Post-call questionnaire
- Scam risk analysis

Never asks users to identify scams themselves.

Instead, guides them with simple questions.

---

## Feature 3

### Smart UPI Protection

Detects

- Fake collect requests
- QR scams
- Refund scams
- Unknown merchants
- Fake payment requests

Explains

"You are being asked to PAY."

instead of

"You received money."

---

## Feature 4

### AI Explainability

Every alert contains

Risk Level

Reason

Simple explanation

Recommended action

Voice explanation

Supported Languages

- Gujarati
- English

---

## Feature 5

### Emergency Recovery Assistant

Provides immediate actions

- Block Number
- Report Cyber Crime
- Contact Bank
- Notify Trusted Family
- Save Evidence

---

## Feature 6

### Safe Scan

Single scanner supporting

- QR Codes
- URLs
- Images
- Screenshots

AI analyses all supported inputs.

---

## Feature 7

### OTP Forwarding Protection

Detects and helps disable

Call forwarding

Unknown forwarding configurations

Potential OTP interception

---

## Feature 8

### Trusted Family Mode

Allows users to add trusted contacts.

When high-risk scam activity is detected,

Rakshak can

- Notify trusted contacts
- Share incident summary
- Help family members assist the user

# 20. Product Promise

Rakshak exists to ensure that no user feels alone when facing a financial scam.

Whether before, during, or after a scam attempt, Rakshak acts as a trusted companion by detecting threats, explaining risks in simple language, and guiding users toward safe decisions.

Our mission is not only to protect users from financial fraud but also to empower them with the confidence and knowledge needed to participate safely in the digital economy.
