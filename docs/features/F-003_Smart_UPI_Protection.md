# F-003

## Smart UPI Protection

**Priority:** P0

**Status:** Planned

### Objective

Protect users from common UPI fraud.

### Description

Analyses UPI payment requests and explains whether money will be credited or debited.

### Detects

- Collect Requests
- Fake Refunds
- Unknown Merchant
- QR Scams
- Fake Payment Requests
- Large First Transactions

### Output

- Risk Level
- AI Explanation
- Recommended Action

### Dependencies

- Notification Listener
- AI Risk Engine
- Rule Engine

### Success Criteria

- Users clearly understand whether they are paying or receiving money.
- Fake requests are detected before payment.
